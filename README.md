# QuickMenu - Instant Digital Menus

QuickMenu is a simple, elegant, and entirely free web application designed to empower any food vendor—small restaurants, cozy cafes, bustling food stalls—to create a beautiful, mobile-friendly digital menu in minutes. It bridges the gap between traditional paper menus and expensive, complex digital ordering systems, offering a professional solution with zero cost and zero complexity.

## The Vision & The Problem

In today's fast-paced world, agility is key for small food businesses. Traditional paper menus are:
*   **Costly to print and reprint.**
*   **Difficult to update** with daily specials or price changes.
*   **Potentially unhygienic.**

While sophisticated digital systems exist, they often come with high setup costs, complicated interfaces, and recurring monthly subscriptions—barriers too high for many small business owners. QuickMenu solves this.

## How It Works: The Solution

QuickMenu is a purely browser-based tool. This means a restaurant owner can do everything from start to finish in a single session, without needing to install any software or create an account.

The user journey is designed for ultimate simplicity:

1.  **Open QuickMenu:** Download the QuickMenu files (or access it online if hosted) and open the `index.html` file in your web browser.
2.  **Build Your Menu:** You're greeted with a clean, intuitive interface.
    *   Add categories like "Appetizers," "Main Courses," "Desserts," "Beverages."
    *   Within each category, add individual menu items: name, a short description, and price.
3.  **See it Live:** As you add or modify items, a live preview of your digital menu updates in real-time on the screen. This preview shows exactly what your customers will see on their smartphones—a clean, readable, and professionally designed menu.
4.  **Generate Your Link:** Once your menu is complete, click "Generate My Menu." The application instantly creates a unique, permanent web link (URL) for your menu. The magic is that the entire menu's data is cleverly encoded directly into this link, making it self-contained.
5.  **Download Your QR Code:** Immediately after generating the link, a "Download QR Code" button appears. Download this high-quality QR code image. Print it and place it on tables, at the counter, or on flyers.
6.  **Customers Scan & View:** When a customer scans the QR code with their smartphone, they are instantly taken to the beautiful digital menu on their own device.

## Key Features

*   **Intuitive Menu Builder:** A simple, form-based interface for adding/editing categories and items (name, description, price).
*   **Real-Time Live Preview:** See your customer-facing menu take shape as you build it.
*   **Instant URL Generation:** Creates a unique and permanent link for the menu with all data encoded within it.
*   **One-Click QR Code Download:** Get a ready-to-print QR code that directs customers to your menu URL.
*   **Easy Editing:** To update the menu, simply visit your unique menu URL. The application will recognize the encoded data, repopulate the menu builder, and allow you to make changes. Generate a new link/QR code if needed (the old link will still work with the old data).
*   **Fully Responsive Design:** The final menu display and the builder interface are designed to look and work great on any device, from a small smartphone to a larger tablet or desktop.
*   **No Installation, No Accounts, No Costs:** Purely browser-based. Completely free to use.
*   **Self-Contained & Portable:** All menu data is in the URL, so there's no database or backend needed.

## How to Use

1.  **Get the Files:** If you've downloaded QuickMenu as a ZIP file, extract all the files (`index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`) into a folder on your computer.
2.  **Open in Browser:** Open the `index.html` file in your preferred web browser (like Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
3.  **Build Your Menu:** Use the interface to add your categories and items.
4.  **Generate & Share:** Click "Generate My Menu" to get your unique URL and download your QR code.
5.  **Update When Needed:** Bookmark your generated menu URL. To make changes, simply open that URL in your browser. The menu builder will load your data. After editing, generate a new URL/QR code to reflect the updates.

QuickMenu is designed to be as simple and accessible as possible, putting digital menu creation in the hands of every food vendor.
